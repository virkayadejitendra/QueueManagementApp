# Queue Management MVP Promotion Plan

## Summary

Promote this MVP as a free pilot for local businesses that manage walk-in queues.

Core message:

```text
Let customers join by QR, see their live status, and reduce crowding near the counter.
```

This is not a finished SaaS launch yet. The goal is to get real owner conversations, run a few free pilots, and learn which features businesses need before they would pay.

## Target Businesses

Start broad, but prioritize businesses where walk-in waiting is visible and painful:

- Restaurants with waiting lists
- Clinics
- Salons
- Service centers
- Small offices with token queues

## Problems To Mention

Use owner-friendly language. Focus on daily pain, not technical features.

- Paper or token queues are hard to manage during rush hours.
- Customers keep asking staff, "How much time?"
- Staff must manually call, skip, restore, or cancel people.
- Crowding near the counter creates pressure.
- Owners do not have a simple live queue display.

## Product Promise

Explain the MVP in one simple flow:

- Customers join the queue from a QR code or link.
- Customers receive a private live status link.
- Staff manage the queue from a dashboard.
- Staff can call, serve, skip, cancel, and restore customers.
- Walk-in customers can still be added manually.
- A display screen can show the current queue.

## Offer

Use this first offer:

```text
Free 7-14 day queue management pilot.
I will help set up your business, generate your QR code, and collect feedback after the pilot.
```

Do not lead with price yet. First get proof that real businesses will use it.

## 14-Day Campaign Plan

### Day 1: Prepare Demo

- Create one demo business account, for example `Demo Restaurant`.
- Add a few sample queue entries.
- Prepare the manager dashboard, customer join page, customer status page, and display screen.

### Day 2: Record Demo Video

Record a 45-60 second phone video showing:

- Customer opens the join link or scans QR.
- Customer joins the queue.
- Manager sees the customer in the dashboard.
- Manager calls the next customer.
- Customer status updates.
- Display screen shows the current token.

### Days 3-14: Post And Message Daily

Post on:

- WhatsApp Status
- LinkedIn
- Facebook local business groups
- Instagram or Reels if the demo video looks clear

Main call to action:

```text
Message me "QUEUE" for free setup.
```

Reply personally to every interested owner. The goal is to start conversations, not collect likes.

## Demo Call Script

Keep the demo short:

1. Ask how they manage queues today.
2. Ask what creates the most pressure for staff or customers.
3. Show customer join.
4. Show manager dashboard.
5. Show customer status.
6. Show display screen.
7. Ask if they would try it for one week.

Close with:

```text
If you are comfortable, I can set up a free pilot for your business and give you a QR code to test with real customers.
```

## Success Criteria

Treat the campaign as successful if, within 14 days, you get:

- 20 or more owner conversations
- 5 or more serious demo requests
- 2 or more businesses willing to run a free pilot
- 1 or more business using it with real customers
- Clear feedback on missing must-have features

## Common Objections And Replies

### "We already use paper tokens."

```text
That makes sense. This pilot is mainly for reducing repeated customer questions and giving staff a simple live queue screen. You can still add walk-ins manually.
```

### "Customers may not scan QR."

```text
That is why the pilot is useful. You can test it with only a few customers first, and staff can still add walk-ins manually when needed.
```

### "Is it paid?"

```text
The pilot is free for 7-14 days. I am collecting real feedback first. If it saves time for your team, then we can discuss a simple monthly price later.
```

### "Is customer data safe?"

```text
The MVP only needs basic queue details. For the pilot, we can keep the information minimal and avoid collecting anything unnecessary.
```

## Notes

- Do not sell this as a perfect enterprise product yet.
- Do not spend heavily on ads before getting organic replies.
- Focus on businesses that have visible waiting and owner access.
- After every demo, write down the owner's exact objection.
- Pricing should come only after at least one or two businesses use it with real customers.
